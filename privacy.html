Jackie Drive Backups

Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026.

Jackie Drive Backups is a personal-use connection operated by Jackie for her own backups. It uses rclone on her Mac to connect to her Google Drive account.

Access and use

The connection requests the Google Drive permission to see, edit, create, and delete files. It uses access to file contents, names, folders, sizes, and other file metadata to copy and verify backups as directed by the owner. It does not request Gmail, Calendar, or Contacts access.

Storage and sharing

Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Authorization credentials and backup logs are stored locally on her Mac. Data is used only for the owner's backup and maintenance work; it is not sold or used for advertising. This website does not display backup files or credentials and does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Control and retention

The owner controls retention of backup files and local logs. She can remove the application's access through her Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing backups; those can be managed directly in Google Drive.

Contact

Use the developer contact email shown on the Google authorization screen for this application.
